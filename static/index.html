Ticket Scalping Definition (Illegal Resale Criteria)

Based on: Performing Arts Act (공연법) Art. 4-2, National Sports Promotion Act (국민체육진흥법) Art. 6-2

  • Illegal Purchase (부정구매): Circumventing or interfering with fair purchasing processes set by the original seller for the purpose of resale, purchasing tickets from the original seller
  • Illegal Sale (부정판매): Selling or brokering tickets without the entrusted seller's consent, habitually or for business, at a price exceeding the purchase price
Penalty: Up to 1 year imprisonment or up to 10,000,000 KRW fine. Plus fines of 2x~50x of total sale amount.

Detection Criteria Used by ScalpHunter

  • Listed price exceeds known face value of the ticket
  • Seller has multiple listings for same event (habitual pattern)
  • Seller account age is recent (created near event announcement)
  • Cross-platform activity detected (same username on multiple platforms)
  • Known scalper keywords: "seat upgrade," "sold out," "limited," "below market"
  • Bulk listing pattern (3+ tickets for same event)

Applicable Ticket Types

COVERED (Reportable)

  • Concert / Performance tickets
  • Sports event tickets (KBO, K-League, etc.)
  • E-sports event tickets
  • Musical / Theater tickets
  • Festival admission tickets

NOT COVERED

  • Movie tickets
  • Accommodation vouchers
  • Fan meetings (general)
  • Gaming events (no competition)

Reporting Procedure

  • Evidence required: screenshot of listing, transaction record, chat logs, payment proof
  • Anonymous reporting is possible
  • Whistleblower reward available upon successful investigation
  • Applies to foreigners using Korean platforms (속지주의 principle)
